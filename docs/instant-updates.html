FORTU CMS · What’s new

Instant Updates

When you change something in the CMS, your screens now find out right away in most cases — instead of waiting for their next scheduled check-in.

Rolling out graduallyNo setup required

What changed

Every screen has always checked in with the CMS every 30 seconds to see if anything changed — that hasn’t gone away, and it never will, because it’s what keeps a screen working even on a bad connection. What’s new is that the CMS can now also reach out to a screen the moment you make a change, instead of leaving it to wait for its next check-in.

In practice, that turns most edits from “update within half a minute” into “update in moments.”

Where you’ll notice it

Assigning a playlist From a screen’s Manage tab, or the quick-assign dropdown on the devices list.
Renaming or relocating a screen Name, location, orientation and timezone changes all reach the screen right away.
Editing what’s inside a playlist Reordering, adding, or removing media updates every screen it plays on.
Changing a daypart schedule Adding, editing, or removing a scheduled override for a screen.

What to expect right now

Rolling out screen by screen

This reaches each screen the next time it reconnects — there’s nothing for you to turn on. A screen that hasn’t picked it up yet simply keeps working exactly as it always has: changes still land within its normal 30-second check-in. Nothing gets slower while this rolls out — some things just get faster, screen by screen.

Questions

Do I need to change any settings?

No. This works automatically in the background — nothing to enable, nothing to configure.

What if a screen is offline when I make a change?

It picks up the change as soon as it reconnects, the same as always. Offline screens have never lost changes — they just catch up a little slower.

Will every screen get this at the same time?

Not necessarily. Screens adopt it individually as they reconnect, so it’s normal to see some screens respond instantly while others still take up to 30 seconds for a little while longer.

Does this change how playlists or schedules work?

No — it only changes how quickly a screen finds out about a change you’ve already made. Everything about how you build playlists and schedules stays the same.
